## LESSONS LEARNED
1. Price based on VALUE to client, not hours to build. A $20K website that brings $2,500/month in new patients pays for itself in 8 months.
2. AI makes John faster and more profitable — it does NOT mean prices should be lower.
3. Full code ownership is a premium feature worth charging MORE for, not a reason to charge less.
4. The fear of hearing "no" was costing more than any actual "no" ever would.
5. At $2,500 per project with no retainer, John needed 44 clients to hit $110K. At proper pricing with retainers, he needs 15-22 clients for $200K+.
6. Med spas spend $8K-$30K/month on marketing. A $6,500 website is less than one month of their Google Ads.
7. 300+ page builds were being sold for $1,000-$2,000. Market value: $18,000-$25,000+. Those clients are now case study opportunities.
8. Monthly retainers with 3-month minimums protect both parties — SEO takes 6-8 weeks to show results.
9. 35% of med spa owners prioritize ROI over price when choosing marketing partners.
10. Almost zero med spas (score 1-2) have good websites. The entire industry is underserved.
