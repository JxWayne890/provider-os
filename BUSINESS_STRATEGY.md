## PRICING PSYCHOLOGY NOTES

1. **Never lead with price.** Lead with the problem (their website issues), then the solution (what you build), then the value (patients they'll gain), THEN the price.

2. **$6,500 is the sweet spot.** Most med spas will land on the Growth package. It's high enough to signal quality but low enough that it's a no-brainer for a business doing $1.5M/year.

3. **The retainer discount makes Option B irresistible.** Saving $1,000 on the project fee by committing to 3 months of retainer feels like free money. In reality, you're gaining $3,000 in retainer revenue for a $1,000 project discount — 3x ROI on the discount.

4. **Full ownership is your closing weapon.** When they compare you to agencies charging $1,500/month for a site they don't own, your model wins every time. "You own the code, you own the hosting, you can walk away anytime and keep everything."

5. **Anchor high, close mid.** If a prospect balks at $12,000 Dominate, the $6,500 Growth package suddenly feels reasonable. Always show Dominate first.

6. **The $1,000/month retainer pitch:** "My retainer costs less than one patient visit. If my SEO brings you even one new patient a month, it's paying for itself. Everything after that is profit."
